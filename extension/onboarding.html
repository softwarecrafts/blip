🔴 Claude Chat Status

This adds a 🔴 / ✅ prefix to your claude.ai chat titles so you can see at a glance which conversations are waiting on you versus done — on desktop and mobile. It works by reading a small status marker that Claude leaves at the end of its replies, which you enable with a one-time preference below.

Step 1 — Add the preference to your Claude profile

Copy this, then paste it into your Claude personal preferences:

Open Claude settings →

In Claude settings, look for the personal-preferences box (often labelled “What personal preferences should Claude consider in responses?”) and paste it there. It appends to anything already in that box.

Step 2 — That's it

From now on, when Claude ends a reply with 🔴, the chat title gets a 🔴 prefix and the toolbar badge counts it. When a chat resolves, it flips to ✅. Click the toolbar icon any time to see your “waiting on you” list.

Tweak behaviour (star mirroring, sweep interval) in Settings. Existing chats stay untouched until Claude leaves a marker in them — nothing to migrate.
