01 — The problem

Your chats have no scope.

So you keep seventeen tabs open as a tracking system — terrified that closing one means losing the thread where the assistant asked you something and is still waiting for an answer.

  • × No way to tell cleared chats from active ones

  • × Tabs-as-todo-list goes dark the moment you restart
  • × None of it follows you to your phone
02 — Signal chain

Three steps. The scope does the watching.

1

Teach the chat to transmit

A one-time paste into your AI preferences. It asks the assistant to end each substantive reply with a tiny 🔴 / ✅ status line. It appends to your existing preferences — nothing is overwritten.

preference prompt 
End every substantive reply with exactly one
status line as the final line:

🔴 Waiting on you: <what you need from me>
✅ Resolved — safe to archive this chat.

Skip it for trivial exchanges. Never start a
line with 🔴 or ✅ for any other purpose.
2

Blip sweeps the signal

From then on, every meaningful answer ends with a status marker — automatically, everywhere you chat, including the mobile app. Blip reads the literal signal the assistant wrote.

…so I'd go with the mid-range supplier. Want me to draft the email?

🔴 Waiting on you: confirm the supplier choice

3

The contact lights your list

Blip prefixes the chat title with 🔴 (active) or ✅ (cleared). It's exact — it reads what the assistant wrote, it never guesses. Because titles live on the server, the markers show up on your phone too.

Small surface. Sharp signal.

A one-click queue

Click the toolbar icon for a tidy list of every chat waiting on you — each a link straight back into the conversation. The badge keeps the active count in view, so nothing drops off the scope.

📱 Syncs to mobile

Markers are part of the title and stored server-side, so they appear in the AI app automatically. Triage the whole scope from the sofa.

⭐ Optional star mirroring

Turn it on and 🔴 active chats get starred — so your Starred section becomes a ready-made queue on every device. Off by default; your manual stars stay yours.

🎯 Exact, never guessed

No fuzzy AI scoring of your chats. Blip matches the literal marker the assistant wrote — deterministic and predictable. A contact is a contact.

⚙️ Yours to tune

A simple settings page: master on/off, star mirroring, and how often Blip re-sweeps for chats cleared on other devices.

04 — Trust

Your chats never leave your browser.

There's no server to send anything to. Blip talks only to your AI provider, using the login you already have — exactly like the website does. No back-channel, no relay, nowhere for a signal to leak.

  • No server. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. There's nowhere for it to go.
  • No analytics, no telemetry. You are not measured or tracked.
  • Local only. Settings and a tiny cache stay on your device.
  • Open source. MIT licensed — read every line before you trust it.
05 — Questions

The fair things to ask.

Will it overwrite my existing AI preferences?

No. You paste the line in yourself, and it appends to whatever you already have. You stay in full control of your own prompt — that's deliberate.

Is my chat data sent anywhere?

No. There's no backend, no analytics, no third parties. The only network requests go to your AI provider, authenticated by your own browser session — the same calls the site already makes.

Does it work on the mobile app?

The markers do. They're part of the chat title, which is stored server-side, so they appear in the app wherever you see your chat list. Blip itself runs on desktop Chrome — it does the sweeping and labelling; your phone just shows the result.

What if the AI site changes and it breaks?

It's possible — Blip uses undocumented endpoints. But it's open source, so it can be fixed, and the failure mode is gentle: at worst a marker goes stale, and you can edit any title back by hand. Stand down, nothing destructive.

Is this made by the AI provider?

No. Blip is an unofficial community tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by any AI provider. Just something built by someone who wanted their chats to have a scope.
