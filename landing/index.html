01 — The problem

Your chats have no inbox.

So you keep seventeen tabs open as a memory system — terrified that closing one means forgetting the thread where Claude asked you something and is still waiting.

  • × No way to tell finished chats from open ones
  • × The thing waiting on you scrolls out of sight
  • × Tabs-as-todo-list breaks the moment you restart
  • × None of it follows you to your phone
02 — How it works

Three steps. One of them is "do nothing."

1

Add one line to your Claude preferences

A one-time paste. It asks Claude to end replies with a tiny 🔴 / ✅ marker. It appends to your existing preferences — nothing is overwritten.

preference prompt 
End every substantive reply with exactly one
status line as the final line:

🔴 Waiting on you: <what you need from me>
✅ Resolved — safe to archive this chat.

Skip it for trivial exchanges. Never start a
line with 🔴 or ✅ for any other purpose.
2

Claude tags its own replies

From then on, every meaningful answer ends with a status marker — automatically, everywhere you chat, including the mobile app.

…so I'd go with the mid-range supplier. Want me to draft the email?

🔴 Waiting on you: confirm the supplier choice

3

Your titles get labelled

The extension reads the marker and prefixes the chat title with 🔴 or ✅. It's exact — it reads what Claude wrote, it never guesses. Because titles live on the server, the labels show up on your phone too.

Small surface. Sharp signal.

A one-click queue

Click the toolbar icon for a tidy list of every chat waiting on you — each a link straight back into the conversation. The badge keeps the count in view.

📱 Syncs to mobile

Labels are part of the title and stored server-side, so they appear in the claude.ai app automatically. Triage from the sofa.

⭐ Optional star mirroring

Turn it on and 🔴 chats get starred — so your Starred section becomes a ready-made queue on every device. Off by default; your manual stars stay yours.

🎯 Exact, never guessed

No fuzzy AI scoring of your chats. It matches the literal marker Claude wrote — deterministic and predictable, every time.

⚙️ Yours to tune

A simple settings page: master on/off, star mirroring, and how often it re-checks chats finished on other devices.

04 — Trust

Your chats never leave your browser.

There's no server to send anything to. The extension talks only to claude.ai, using the login you already have — exactly like the website does.

  • No server. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. There's nowhere for it to go.
  • No analytics, no telemetry. You are not measured.
  • Local only. Settings and a tiny cache stay on your device.
  • Open source. MIT licensed — read every line before you trust it.
05 — Questions

The fair things to ask.

Will it overwrite my existing Claude preferences?

No. You paste the line in yourself, and it appends to whatever you already have. You stay in full control of your own prompt — that's deliberate.

Is my chat data sent anywhere?

No. There's no backend, no analytics, no third parties. The only network requests go to claude.ai, authenticated by your own browser session — the same calls the site already makes.

Does it work on the mobile app?

The labels do. They're part of the chat title, which is stored server-side, so they appear in the app wherever you see your chat list. The extension itself runs on desktop Chrome — it does the labelling; your phone just shows the result.

What if claude.ai changes and it breaks?

It's possible — the extension uses undocumented endpoints. But it's open source, so it can be fixed, and the failure mode is gentle: at worst a label is stale, and you can edit any title back by hand. Nothing destructive.

Is this made by Anthropic?

No. It's an unofficial community tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Just something built by someone who wanted their chats to have an inbox.
